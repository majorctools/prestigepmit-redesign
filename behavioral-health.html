The whole claim, not just submission.

Each stage has an owner and a next action, from the first eligibility check through payment reconciliation.

  • Eligibility reviewCheck coverage details before the claim moves forward.
  • Coding reviewConnect documentation and coding before submission.
  • Claim submissionSend electronic or paper claims according to payer requirements.
  • Denial follow-upReview denials, correct issues and manage appeals.
  • Payment postingPost and reconcile payments against the practice’s records.
  • Performance reportingReview billing activity and open follow-up with the practice.
